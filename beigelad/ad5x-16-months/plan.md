## Shoot list (film before cleaning anything)
1. Cold open: the printer as it is. Say the hook.
2. History, about 30s: how long owned, total print time if the printer shows it (else say you
   don't know), the clog from the 91k video, the nozzle swap.
3. Every check: before close-up, what I did, after close-up.
   - [ ] Rods/rails: white paper towel wipe. Show what comes off.
   - [ ] Grease: lead screws and guide rails per the wiki. Name the product.
   - [ ] Belts: pluck each before and after. Show how you judged "taut but not overly tight".
   - [ ] Nozzle, wiper, cutter: what built up.
   - [ ] IFS: dust and filament bits in the paths.
   - [ ] Build plate: condition after 16 months.
4. Keep the mistakes in.
5. Proof: same test print before and after (same file, same filament), side by side, and the
   sound. If nothing changed, say so.
6. Sign-off: "so there you go", what I'll do now and how often.
7. Music: only under the before/after timelapse.
