## How I actually talk

Precise. Slightly dry. Curious. Short sentences. One idea per video, one hook.

- Lead with the named effect or the receipt, never a setup clause.
- Every claim carries a receipt: author, year, venue, one-line finding. No receipt, cut the line.
- Say the limit of the finding out loud. The ugly detail is allowed.
- Specific beats dramatic. Promise the turn, not the topic.
  Weak: "The Psychology of Overthinking". Better: "You Are Not Overthinking. You Are Bargaining."

Example hook lines in this voice:
- "You re-read the text. That is not love. That is variable reward."
- "Willpower is not a muscle. That finding did not replicate."
- "In 2014 people shocked themselves rather than sit alone with their thoughts."

NOT FILLED IN: transcripts of real videos. Paste the best one or three here when they exist.

## Words I never use

- Em dashes. Exclamation points.
- "Have you ever", "In this video", "Let's dive in", "What if I told you"
- "Studies show" with no study
- "your mental health journey", "it's okay to feel"
- "streamline", "optimize", "innovative", "unlock", "game-changer"
- Fake countdown urgency. Therapist register. Headspace register.

## Words I do use

The name of the effect. The author and the year. Plain verbs.

NOT FILLED IN: personal phrases and tics. Add them from real transcripts.

## What I will not claim

- No invented citations, numbers, metrics or testimonials. Every number has a URL or DOI in Sources.
- No sources recalled from memory. Search first.
- No diagnosing the viewer. No treatment advice.
- A beat that touches clinical territory gets a one-line not-advice card in the outro.

## My format

- Short: 35 to 55 seconds, hook plus 2 beats plus payoff, about 90 to 130 spoken words.
- Long: 8 to 14 minutes, hook plus 5 to 8 beats plus payoff, about 1,200 to 1,800 words.
- Explainer cut: every receipt becomes an on-screen card.
- Spoken hook lands within 3 seconds, or a 1-second picture plus the line by second 8.
- Thumbnail: 3 to 5 words, no question unless answered in the first 8 seconds, no emoji.
  Explainer look: cream paper, one figure, one rust accent word.

NOT FILLED IN: on camera or voiceover, intro or none, swearing, upload rhythm.
