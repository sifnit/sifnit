## Who I am talking to

Someone who owns a specific budget printer (Flashforge AD5X, Anycubic Kobra 3, Creality K1C or
K2 Plus, Snapmaker U1) and has a problem with it right now: a clog, a snapped belt, a wobble, a
purge setting that will not stick. They searched the model name plus the symptom.

NOT FILLED IN: confirm this with the user, and whether beginners or tinkerers.

## What I will not claim

- No print-quality, speed or reliability numbers that were not measured on camera.
- No "this fixes every clog" claims. Say what fixed it on this printer, and say if it later
  stopped working.
- Disclose any free or sponsored printer. NOT FILLED IN: does the channel get review units?

## My format

- Long: fix videos, 3 to 9 minutes. They earn the views (91k, 26k, 17k, 13k, 13k). Reviews and
  news earn far less (the 23-minute AD5X review: 1.4k; Kobra S1 news: 1.6k).
- Shorts: quick swaps, upgrades and prints coming off the plate, cut from or around long videos.
- Titles: model name plus symptom, under 55 characters, one or two words in caps for emphasis.
- Thumbnails: the printer part close up, 1 to 3 big words ("Clog", "Add text", "It won"),
  sometimes a hand-drawn word. Readable at feed size.
- On camera: sometimes, hands and machine mostly. Mic: Ulanzi A100 lapel (from a Short).

NOT FILLED IN: intro or none, swearing, music, sign-off, upload schedule.
