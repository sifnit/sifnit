No monthly EFTPOS rental.

A simple counter system for small shops. Scan at the register or with a phone, take cash or card, and see your sales and stock on screen. It runs on a small computer in your shop.

No monthly rental

Card payments go through your own Square reader. You pay its per-sale fee only; no terminal rental, no lock-in contract.

Easy for staff

Big buttons, PIN login, clear change-due screen. New staff are serving customers in minutes.

Stock on your phone

Receive deliveries and do stocktakes by scanning tags with a phone. No paperwork, no app to install.

GST done right

Prices include 10% GST, receipts are tax invoices, cash rounds to 5c, and GST totals are ready for your BAS.

Reports on screen

Today's takings, cash vs card, top sellers, low stock and stock value. Nothing to print.

Your data stays with you

Everything is stored on your own computer in the shop, with one-click backup.

Your Brand POS What's inside

At the counter

  • Scan by bench scanner, webcam or phone, or search
  • Cash, card and split payments, with change shown
  • Australian 5c cash rounding handled automatically
  • Receipts as GST tax invoices, printed or on screen
  • Refunds that put items back into stock
  • Warns when a price tag is out of date

Stock

  • Add products in seconds; codes created for you
  • Receive deliveries by scanning with a phone
  • Stocktake from a phone: scan a tag, type the count
  • Low-stock alerts and full stock history per product
  • Supplier codes kept on every product

Tags and scanning

  • QR price tags from your label printer or A4 sheets
  • A phone's camera opens stocktake straight from a tag
  • NFC tags read on a counter pad, any way up Coming
  • Hands-free counter: pad and camera Coming

Reports and control

  • Sales by day or hour, top sellers, takings by cashier
  • Cash vs card totals and GST collected
  • End-of-day cash count shows over or short
  • Owner and cashier PIN logins, plus an activity log

Stock work moves to your phone

Point your phone at a tag and the stocktake screen opens, ready for the count. Receiving a delivery works the same way. No handheld terminal to buy.

Every tag carries the product code, supplier code and price. The register always charges the current price.
