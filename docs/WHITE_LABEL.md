# White-label POS: rundown

**What it is:** a POS and stock system for small Australian shops, sold under
your brand. It runs on a small computer in each shop and works on the shop's
Wi-Fi. There's no monthly EFTPOS rental and no subscription is required.

## What the customer gets
- Browser-based register, phone stock scanning and stocktake
- QR + NFC tags; hands-free counter scanning (NFC pad for small items, camera
  for large)
- Cash, card on a Square reader (the shop's own account), split payments
- GST tax-invoice receipts, refunds, on-screen reports, cash-up
- Data stays in the shop
- Add-ons later: supplier ordering, electronic shelf labels, integrated card reader

## What you sell
- **Setup package:** install, load products, print tags, train staff
- **Hardware bundle:** mini PC, TSC label printer, NFC reader, webcam, handheld scanner
- **Optional support plan:** updates, backups, help line. Keep it optional so
  "no monthly fees" stays true
- **Consumables:** tag rolls

## Before selling
1. Trial in your own shop first.
2. Brand settings: product name, logo, colours (today only the shop name is configurable).
3. One-step installer, start on boot, automatic off-site backups, update method.
4. Trusted HTTPS certificate per shop (no phone warnings).
5. Security review of the finished product.
6. Customer terms, privacy policy (if receipts are emailed), warranty wording
   that fits Australian Consumer Law. Get legal advice.
7. Keep open-source notices for the bundled QR libraries (qrcode-generator MIT,
   jsQR Apache-2.0; both allow commercial use).
8. Set pricing after the trial, once setup time and support load are known.

## Why shops would buy it
- No monthly EFTPOS rental or POS subscription
- Card fees about 1.6% through their own Square account
- Simple: phone-based stock work, hands-free counter
- Their data stays with them, on their own computer
